# Manual accessibility checklist (WCAG 2.2 AA target)

- [ ] Keyboard-only navigation across header, explorer, stack, sample form
- [ ] Visible focus on all interactive controls
- [ ] Heading order on homepage and chapter pages
- [ ] Form errors associated with fields
- [ ] Contrast on paper and ink surfaces
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` disables non-essential motion
- [ ] Skip link works
- [ ] Zoom 200% usable
- [ ] Screen reader announces stack tab selection
- Do not claim full compliance from automation alone.
