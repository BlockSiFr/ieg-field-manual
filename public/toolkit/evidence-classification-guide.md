# The Consequence Evidence Ladder

Published by BlockSiFr. Free reference. CC BY 4.0 with attribution.
https://ieg.blocksifr.com/toolkit

Use evidence classification for **each material claim**, not just the overall project.

| State | What it supports | What it does not support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hypothesis | A plausible causal pathway, based on stated assumptions | Actual exploitability, realized damage, avoided loss, or savings |
| Modeled | An outcome under explicit assumptions or a simulation setup | Field performance, operational quality, or a verified financial return |
| Observed | An outcome captured in a defined authorized test or operational interval | Generalization outside the scope, independent reproduction, or universal safety |
| Independently verified | A particular claim examined by a separately accountable reviewer with access to suitable evidence | Claims beyond the audit/test scope or perpetual validity |

## Minimum evidence register
For every claim, record:
1. Claim and precise scope.
2. Protected outcome and potential consequence.
3. Baseline or counterfactual and measurement boundary.
4. Source artifacts, hashes, dates, and who collected them.
5. Relevant controls, trust boundaries, and counterarguments.
6. Observations, outcomes, and limitations.
7. Reproduction instructions when authorization and confidentiality permit.

## Three frequent errors
**Severity is not impact.** A generic rating cannot prove the affected service or loss.
**Permission is not admissibility.** A credential may enable an action that should not be permitted in context.
**Reduction is not savings.** Less compute at the proxy is not necessarily less facility energy, cooling, or expense.

## Decision questions
- What would falsify the conclusion?
- What is measured rather than inferred?
- Can another person examine the chain without trusting the author?
- Does the proposed action change an irreversible outcome?
- Which uncertainty matters enough to block a decision?

This guide does not substitute for an authorized assessment, independent audit, or engineering measurement.
