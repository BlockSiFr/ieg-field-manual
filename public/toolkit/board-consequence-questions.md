# Seven questions boards and operating teams should ask before approving spend

BlockSiFr / Consequence Intelligence
Free to reuse with attribution under CC BY 4.0

1. **What specific harm or lost opportunity are we paying to prevent or recover?** Name the affected business outcome.
2. **How could that outcome actually happen?** Show the causal path and what must be true.
3. **What have we observed, and what is only assumed?** Label hypothesis, model, observation, and external verification.
4. **What is the exposure under a credible counterfactual?** Document assets, timeframe, baseline, and unit; do not invent a dollar value.
5. **Who or what is authorized to change the result?** Specify operating constraints and authority boundaries.
6. **What experiment or observation would change our decision?** Favor bounded, reversible, authorized validation.
7. **What will we show after implementation?** Require evidence of outcomes and limitations, not only implementation milestones.
